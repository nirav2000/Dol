How it works

1point for every pair you find
+2bonus for finding all of them

Example: 12 has three pairs, 1 × 12, 2 × 6 and 3 × 4. Every number in this game has at least four.

The pair 1 × the number counts too. Order does not matter, so 6 × 4 and 4 × 6 are one pair.
